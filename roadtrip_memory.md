# Cross-Country Solo Road Trip: Context & Memory State

## 1. Trip Overview & Traveler Profile
* **Route:** New York City (NYC) ➜ Los Angeles (LA).
* **Direction:** East to West (one-way).
* **Duration:** 10 Days / 9–10 Nights (~3,000 miles total including detours).
* **Departure Date:** Early November 2026 (immediately following the NYC Marathon on Nov 1).
* **Traveler Format:** Strictly solo.
* **Travel Philosophy:** Active adventure, nature-focused, waking up outdoors, authentic local food/breweries, independent coffee roasters, zero chain restaurants.
* **Physical Considerations:** Post-marathon physical fatigue/recovery. Daily driving must be bounded (~4 to 4.5 hours typical, single big transit day capped at 8 hours) with required walking/stretching intervals.

---

## 2. Accommodation & Sleeping Strategy
* **Format:** Hybrid setup (~6 nights Tier-2 boutique/business hotels, ~4 nights vehicle car-camping).
* **Car Camping Locations:** National Parks (reserved tent pads) and Bureau of Land Management (BLM) dispersed land (New Mexico, Utah, Arizona).
* **Pre-Race NYC Accommodation:**
  * Target areas: Upper West Side (near Central Park finish) or Long Island City (LIC).
  * Selected benchmark: Boro Hotel (LIC) or private room micro-hotels (Pod 51, Pod 39).
  * Hard Rule: No shared hostel dorms prior to race day due to sleep disruption risks.

---

## 3. Vehicle & Equipment Specifications
* **Target Category:** Midsize SUV or Standard/Full-Size SUV with verified flat-folding rear seats.
* **Booking Mechanism:** Utilize National Emerald Club, Hertz Ultimate Choice, or Avis Preferred Select & Go to hand-pick the exact vehicle in the airport garage.
* **Target Models:**
  * Top picks: Toyota RAV4, Subaru Outback (ideal flat cargo depth ~75 in / 191 cm).
  * Acceptable 3-Row upgrades: Ford Explorer, Chevy Traverse (~81 in / 206 cm cargo depth).
  * Hard Rejections: Chevrolet Trax (subcompact, too short), Chevrolet Equinox (cargo floor only 63 in / 160 cm), Mazda CX-5 (steep incline angle), Jeep Compass.
* **Camping Kit to Purchase in U.S. (Budget ~$200):**
  * Insulated sleeping pad (high R-value ≥ 4.5 or 3" memory foam topper).
  * Cold-weather sleeping bag (rated 15°F to 35°F / -9°C to 2°C).
  * Travel thermos + portable pour-over/drip coffee gear.
  * DIY window insulation (roll of Reflectix + shears) for thermal barrier and 100% blackout privacy.

## 4. Financial Baseline

  * Vehicle Rental + One-Way Drop Fee: ~$1,400 – $1,500.

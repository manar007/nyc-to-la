## 2. Vehicle, Gear & Car-Camping Strategy

* **Target Models in the Midsize SUV Aisle:**
  * **Top Pick:** **Nissan Rogue** (raise the "Divide-N-Hide" dual cargo floor panel to the upper position to create a dead-flat ~72 in / 183 cm sleeping deck with rear seats folded), **Subaru Outback** (~75 in flat), or **Toyota RAV4**.

* **Day 1 Las Vegas Gear Provisioning (`Mon Nov 2, 12:00 PM – 1:15 PM`):**
  * **Walmart Supercenter** (`6005 S Eastern Ave, Las Vegas, NV 89119` — 8 mins east of rental car center) and/or **REI Henderson** (`2220 Village Walk Dr, Henderson, NV 89052` — 12 mins away).
  * **Kit to Pick Up:**
    * Cold-weather sleeping bag rated `0°F to 15°F (-18°C to -9°C)` (mandatory if car-camping at Bryce Canyon `-6°C` or Grand Canyon `-2°C`).
    * Insulated sleeping pad (`R-value ≥ 4.5`) or 3" memory foam topper + full-size pillow.
    * Compact butane/propane camp stove + 1 fuel canister + kettle/pot + travel thermos.
    * 2.5-gallon (10L) water jug + 2 flats of electrolyte water + trail snacks/fruit.
    * Microfiber beach towel + swim trunks (for Laguna Beach, Crystal Cove, and Malibu ocean swims on Days 8–10).
    * Headlamp (with spare batteries for sunrise/sunset canyon hikes) + baby wipes + Reflectix window roll.

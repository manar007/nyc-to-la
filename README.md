# LAS → CANYONS → LAX (Southwest & Pacific Coast Navigator)

Mobile-first road trip navigator for an **11-day / 10-night post-NYC Marathon adventure** (`Mon Nov 2 – Thu Nov 12, 2026`): flying from **JFK to Las Vegas (`AA 2356`)**, exploring **7 Southwest Parks & Canyons** (Valley of Fire, Zion, Bryce Canyon, Buckskin Gulch, Page / Horseshoe Bend / Antelope Canyon, Monument Valley, Grand Canyon South Rim, and Sedona) followed by **3 days on the Southern California Coast** (Laguna Beach, Crystal Cove, Venice Beach, Santa Monica, Malibu Point Dume & El Matador sea caves, and Griffith Observatory) before flying out of **LAX (`Lufthansa LH 457`)**.

## Features

- **Mobile-First Daily Guides (Days 1–11):** Hour-by-hour timetables, daylight/sunset windows, day/night & ocean water temperatures, time-zone change alerts, and America the Beautiful Pass / permit rules.
- **1-Tap Road Navigation:** Direct buttons on every day and stop to launch turn-by-turn directions in Google Maps, trail maps in AllTrails, and hotel/campground/BLM pins.
- **All Options Preserved:** Keeps multiple hiking options (Option A / B / C), local independent coffee/restaurant stops, and both Hotel & SUV Car-Camping options open for every day so you can decide on the road.
- **Interactive Marathon + Road Trip Packing List:** Persistent checklist (`localStorage`) covering NYC Marathon Race Day (3:20 target), Post-Race Flight & Recovery, Canyon & Slot Hiking (-6°C Bryce freeze & 10°C Narrows), Day-1 Las Vegas Walmart/REI SUV Car-Camping buy list, and Pacific Ocean swimming & documents.
- **Confirmed Bookings Vault:** Quick-reference tab with flight numbers, SUV rental pickup/drop-off addresses (`7135 Gilespie St LAS` → `5251 W 98th St LAX`), Night 1 hotel (`La Quinta Inn La Verkin`), and pass rules.
